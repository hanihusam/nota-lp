# Pricing study: free forever, paid for the cloud

Read from the live pricing pages on 2026-11-06.

## Obsidian (`obsidian.md/pricing`)

- Headline: "Free without limits." No sign-up, no feature limits on the app.
- Paid items are **services**, priced one by one: Sync ($4/user/month,
  billed annually) and Publish ($8/site/month, billed annually).
- Optional supporter licenses: Catalyst (one-time) and Commercial (yearly).
  Commercial use is encouraged, not required.
- Seven-day refunds on services. Data stays on the device unless you use
  Sync, which is end-to-end encrypted.

## Cap (`cap.so/pricing`)

- Headline: start free, pay when you need commercial rights or the cloud.
- **Free:** personal use, Studio Mode, 4K export, and a taste of the cloud
  (25 shareable links a month, up to 5 minutes each).
- **Desktop License:** $29/year or $58 once. Commercial rights, still local.
- **Cap Pro:** $12/user/month, or $8.16 billed annually. Unlimited cloud,
  AI summaries, custom domain, password-protected shares, analytics, teams.
- Enterprise is self-serve add-ons on top of Pro, not a sales tier.

## The pattern

1. Everything that runs on your device is free, with no time or count limit.
2. What costs the company money to run (servers, storage, sending) is paid.
3. The free plan includes a small, real taste of the paid service, so people
   meet it before they need it.
4. "Cancel anytime" and "your data stays yours" sit right under the button.

## Proposed for Nota

**Free, forever.** No account needed.

- Unlimited proposals and clients
- Reusable blocks, automatic totals and tax
- Branded PDF export
- Works offline, keyboard-first
- A taste of Pro: 3 e-signature requests a month

**Pro.** For proposals that live online.

- Unlimited e-signature requests, with a signed PDF and audit trail
- Live proposal links with "viewed" alerts and reminders
- Sync and backup across devices
- Custom domain and no Nota branding on shared links
- Collect a deposit when the client signs

The Pro price shown in the designs is a placeholder until it is decided. For
scale, Cap Pro is $8.16 a month billed annually.

Any legal claim about e-signatures (for example, that they are binding under
the US ESIGN Act or EU eIDAS) needs review before it ships.
